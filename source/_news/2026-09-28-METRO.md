The International Image Interoperability Framework Consortium is delighted to announce the addition of the [Metropolitan New York Library Council (METRO)](https://metro.org) as an Associate member. 

METRO is a non-profit service provider that helps libraries, archives, and museums excel at their work. METRO works to create a sustainable culture of creativity, collaboration, and open exchange for libraries, archives, museums, and cultural institutions in the Metropolitan New York region and around the world. METRO accomplishes its mission through leadership, grantmaking, resource sharing, professional learning, research, technology services, creative practice, and more. Over 200 libraries, archives, museums, and cultural heritage institutions in New York City and surrounding areas are [METRO members](https://metro.org/join/#members).

“METRO is an important hub of the library, archives, and museum and sits in one of the richest aggregation of such institutions in the world,” said Martin Kalfatovic, Managing Director of the IIIF Consortium. “METRO’s work on the Archipelago Commons is an important IIIF repository platform supporting the community and their digital collections.” 

The [Digital Services Team at METRO](https://metro.org/services/digital-services/) supports the development and implementation of [Archipelago Commons](https://docs.archipelago.nyc/1.7.0/), or simply Archipelago, an open source repository platform. There are over 30+ production deployments of Archipelago in the United States and internationally. IIIF APIs and IIIF compliant viewer customizations are woven into Archipelago’s architecture and functionality. METRO’s Digital Services Team and Archipelago repository users have been participating in IIIF community meetings, committees, and conferences for many years.

METRO's Executive Director Nate Hill asserts, "METRO’s staff, member institutions, and Archipelago partners are thrilled to formalize METRO’s connection with the IIIF Consortium. We look forward to continuing to contribute to the advancement of IIIF usage, education, and invaluable impact across our professional fields and communities."


**About the International Image Interoperability Framework**

IIIF is a set of open standards for delivering high-quality, attributed digital objects online at scale. It’s also an international community developing and implementing the IIIF APIs. IIIF is backed by a consortium of leading cultural institutions. In addition to supporting the IIIF community financially, these institutions contribute to the APIs, Community Groups, and more.
